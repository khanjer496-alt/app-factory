# Asset credits

## Food photography

`public/meals/*.webp` (68 dishes) and `public/scenes/hero.webp` are Diet Box's own menu photos, taken from the restaurant's talabat listing (vendor 680421, "DIET BOX", Ajman). They were cropped square and converted to WebP at 720 px (1200 px for the hero).

Before launch, the client must:

- confirm Diet Box owns these photos and may use them on its own website and apps. A studio shoot arranged through a delivery platform can carry usage limits.
- supply the original high-resolution files. The talabat copies top out at about 1,600 px.
- reshoot or replace the three photos that don't match the yellow-background set: Chicken Lasagna, Low-Carb Chicken Salad, and (if drinks are added) the can shots.

The Unsplash placeholder photography used earlier in the redesign has been removed.

## Logo

`public/brand/*` is derived from the logo files published on dietbox.ae (`/static/images/logo.png`, `/static/images/logo-yellow.png`). It was trimmed and recoloured to white, and the emblem was cropped. It remains the client's trademark.
