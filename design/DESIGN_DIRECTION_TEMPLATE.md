## Explicit anti-patterns

- Do not mix unrelated component aesthetics.
- Do not use generic AI gradients/robot imagery unless the brand specifically requires them.
- Do not sacrifice usability for visual effects.

## Component-source plan

- Existing local components first.
- Component Gallery for interaction reference.
- 21st.dev for copy/adapt source when needed.
- beUI only for selective polished interactions.
- Agentation for final human visual QA.
